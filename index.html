My Unofficial Local Guide to Park Slope

Brew Memories

Looking for a good cup of coffee, or a GREAT bubble tea? Brew Memories is the spot for you. With a beautiful brick interior, mellow lamps and lights, and desks and couches, it's a lovely place for those who need a place to work or relax. Brew Memories lives up to it's name, as I've had many beauitful moments and expieriences with my friends and family here. This place is just such a vibe, and feels straight out of one of those lo-fi cafe videos.

If you're looking for drink recommendatons, I'd go with a brown sugar milk tea with boba, or a mango green tea if you want to keep it simple.



Haab

The best Mexican food in Park Slope awaits you at Haab. I very firmly believe that Haab has the best burrito in all of Brooklyn, no, New York as a whole. It's their title burrito, "The Haab Burrito" and it's around 14 dollars. The place has both indoor and outdoor seating and is a fun time all around for friend and family gatherings.
